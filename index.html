Welcome

SG16 Children World is a safe, bright space for kids and families. Meet Robo — a friendly AI computer helper who can help with homework, stories, science facts, feelings, and fun puzzles.

For parents: Children under 13 should use this app with a parent or guardian. Setup takes one minute — a quick math check, a PIN, and an age tier.
Privacy: Sessions use a random ID and settings stored in your browser only (sessionStorage). No camera. No face scan. No account required to try.

What Robo is

Robo is a computer helper in a cartoon robot body — powered by SG16 Mistral X, Saif Tech Global’s own cloud brain.

Emergency: Robo is not for emergencies. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number immediately.
Kid rule: Never share real names, addresses, phone numbers, school names, or passwords with Robo or anyone online.

Three age worlds

Parents choose the right tier so content stays age-appropriate.

🤖

6–11 · Robo Kids

Simple words, short answers, extra safety, and video Robo with thinking & speaking modes.

⚡

12–17 · Teen Zone

Clearer explanations for homework, hobbies, and growing-up questions — still safe and legal.

🎓

18+ · Family adult

Standard assistant mode for older family members — same safety backbone.

How to start

Tap Start above to enter the app. A parent completes setup first.

1 · Parent gate Quick math question + 4-digit PIN so only a grown-up opens settings.
2 · Choose tier Pick 6–11, 12–17, or 18+ and an optional nickname.
3 · Chat with Robo Ask questions, get stories, learn — Robo thinks, then types the answer.

Privacy policy

Who we are

The data controller for this website is Saif Tech Global LLC, 8206 Louisiana Blvd NE, Ste A #10595, Albuquerque, NM 87113, USA. SG16 Children World is a family-facing product on the SG16 platform. Questions: contact@saiftechglobal.com.

What we collect

  • Session settings — age tier, optional nickname, voice/mic preferences, and a random session ID stored in your browser (sessionStorage).
  • Chat messages — text you send to Robo so the SG16 cloud brain can generate a reply.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) for security and reliability.
  • We do not require a child’s legal name, email, phone number, photo, or precise location to use the demo.

How we use data

  • To provide age-appropriate AI replies through SG16 Mistral X (api.mistralbrain.com via sg16engine.com).
  • To apply safety rules and block harmful requests.
  • To keep the service secure and fix errors.
  • We do not sell children’s personal data. We do not use this demo for behavioural advertising.

Children under 13

A parent or guardian should complete setup (parent gate + consent checkboxes) before a child uses Robo. We design the experience to minimise personal data. If you believe a child shared sensitive information in chat, use Reset in the app and contact us so we can review server-side handling where applicable.

Retention & your rights

Browser session data clears when you reset or close the tab (sessionStorage). Server-side chat handling follows SG16 platform retention limits for operational and safety logs. US and international users may have rights to access, correction, or deletion — contact us with your request. See also Saif Tech Global Privacy Policy.

Safety & community rules

Always

  • Treat Robo like a helpful tutor — not a replacement for parents, teachers, or emergency services.
  • Never share full name, home address, phone number, school name, passwords, or photos of ID documents.
  • Stop and tell a trusted adult if a reply feels scary, rude, or wrong.
  • Voice output and microphone require parent approval in the Parent Panel.

Never use Robo for

  • Medical emergencies, mental-health crises, or situations where someone is in immediate danger.
  • Illegal activity, weapons, drugs, bullying, or sexual content involving minors.
  • Cheating on exams in ways your school forbids — ask a teacher what help is allowed.

Age tiers

6–11: simplest language and strongest safety filters. 12–17: teen-appropriate guidance with continued safeguards. 18+: adult family mode — still subject to platform safety and law. Choosing the wrong tier may expose a child to unsuitable content; parents are responsible for correct setup.
